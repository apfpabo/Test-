- **Code review round (10 Nov 2026):** what was found, fixed and left for the owner is in [`docs/ROADMAP.md`](docs/ROADMAP.md) ("Code review and bug hunt"). Every fix has a check in `tests/review_test.tscn`; add to it when you fix a defect, and keep its sections counted (a script error ends a section early, and the test fails if a section did not reach its end). `tests/soak_test.tscn -- [days] [origin] [navy] [seed] [v]` plays an unattended campaign (real weather, battles fought by the simulation, a flag, a port, divisions, a tow, strikes, the bomb, saves and loads) and checks the state every day: run a year of it after any change to `campaign.gd`, `flag.gd`, `structures.gd` or `diplomacy.gd`. Every test writes the one save file (`user://iron_meridian_save.json`), so tests that save and load fail falsely when run side by side unless each has its own `XDG_DATA_HOME` (`tools/run_tests.sh` does that). `tests/chart_perf.tscn` and `tests/battle_perf.tscn` time the chart and a ten-a-side battle (headless: script time alone).
- **Things the review settled, to keep:** fleets that have met each other use their own timer `cc` (`cool` is only for contact with the player); a ship bought from a yard carries `paid` (what was put into her: resale and refit refunds are capped by it); a stormed port's structures have `militia` (men who are not yours to embark); `world_map._user_pause` remembers that the player paused the clock; the fleet's size and supply are counted with `Campaign.all_ships()`; `Campaign.fname(f)` gives a fleet's name inside a sentence; the chart's top bar, telegraph and tabs refresh five times a second (`_hud_t`), not every frame, and colour overrides go through `_tc`. In `Campaign.update` nothing is worked out per fleet per tick that can be worked out once (`war`, `ky`, cached `msp`/`mtr`, `route_remaining`'s tail).
- **Decided by the owner after the review:** sighting follows the captain (`Campaign.watch`, `your_watch`, `fleet_watch`; a fleet's `capt`, set in `spawn_fleet`): never give one side a fixed sighting distance the other does not have. `rest_in_port` runs `Campaign.update` with `G.docked` set, so the world moves and no contact is made. Use `Campaign.known_pos(f)` for anything that marks or steers for a fleet the player may not have in sight. `Structures.troops()` and `troop_capacity()` are the soldiers and berths in company; a detached main body keeps its own in its fleet's `troops` (`troops_all()` for pay and food). After removing a ship call `Campaign.trim_cargo()` as well as `Structures.trim_troops()`. `Flag.treaty(fac)` must stop every hostile act of that power. `tools/run_tests.sh <log dir> [tests]` runs the headless tests four at a time, each with its own save folder.
- **The Command pages are a drawer** (`world_map.show_command`, `drawer_open`, `close_drawer`): docked above the tab bar, not modal, the campaign not paused and `panel_open` false while it is up. Pages re-render through `_rerender()`; nothing in them may assume the clock is stopped. `UI.bar` keeps its own height in a row.
- **Nuclear plants and the standing ledger:** a structure of kind `nuclear` (`Structures.plants_of`, `stock`, `can_answer`, `_place_plants`; art `InstallationArt._nuclear`); the strike view is `scripts/nuke_view.gd` (loaded by path, not `class_name`: a new global class is not registered until the editor re-imports). Whenever standing changes, call `Campaign.rep_note("<reason>")` after it so the ledger (`G.repLog`, shown on hover in the standing list) can say why.
- **Supply** ([`docs/SUPPLY.md`](docs/SUPPLY.md)) replaces the cap of ten: before giving the player a ship ask `Campaign.can_add_ship(design id)` ("" means yes); `supply_need`/`supply_cap`/`supply_over`, `rdy_cap()` (the best readiness an overstretched fleet can hold), `SHIPS_MAX` (20) and `BATTLE_MAX` (10: `battle_setup` sends only the first ten not in reserve).
- **Refits by weight** ([`docs/REFIT.md`](docs/REFIT.md)): `BattleSim.loading(design, fit, up)` is the ledger (tons against the standard fit and margin, topweight, and the speed, helm and spread multipliers; `ok` false if the yard would refuse). A new weapon needs an entry in `BattleSim.TONS`, a new system `tons` and `top` in `UPGRADES`; every design's standard fit in every era must stay within weight and topweight (review_test checks it). Every ship of either side is reckoned (`ld_speed`, `ld_turn`, `ld_spread` on a `CShip`), and other navies refit too (`Campaign.make_variant`, a ship's `up` on either side). A refit is done at once and costs readiness (`Campaign.refit_done(s, tons moved)`); chart speed follows loading through `Campaign.load_speed(s)` (cached as `ldv`: call `Campaign.ship_refitted(s)` after changing a fit or a system). Armour by place, machinery for a design speed and trim are left open by the owner: do not add them unasked.
- **The economy** ([`docs/ECONOMY.md`](docs/ECONOMY.md)): a port's `short` (per good) and `why`; raise or lower it only through `Campaign.shortage(p, goods, amount, why)`; `Campaign.supply_mult(p, gid)` is everything the world does to a price (war demand, shortage, the flag's works in `G.works`), `econ_day` runs once a day, and a convoy that is destroyed must go through `Campaign.convoy_lost(f)`. Anything that shows or uses a price goes through `world_map.port_price`.
- **Smoke, damage and formations in battle**: smoke is `BattleSim.screens` (round cells; `make_smoke`, `smoke_blocks(a, b)`); anything that asks whether a ship can be seen must go through `visible_to` or `eye_sees`, and anything that hides enemies on screen must test `sim.limited() or not sim.screens.is_empty()`. A ship's `steer` and `list` change how she turns and how fast she goes; `v_cmd` is a speed a station-keeping ship holds (reset each `ai_ship`). A ship of yours may have her own order (`ord`, with `ord_pt` for "goto" and `ord_tgt` for "torp") and her own hold-fire (`hold`): read a ship's order with `ord_of(s)` and her hold-fire with `holding(s)`, never `order` or `hold_fire` directly; the ships picked out in the battle screen are `battle.sel`. Orders: engage, escort, line, abreast, screen, retreat, goto, torp (`station(s)` gives a ship's place in the flagship's frame, x ahead, y to starboard; `keep_station`).
- **Star shell and damage control**: `BattleSim.flares` (`fire_star`, `star_lit(p)`; a ship under a flare at night is seen as by day, through `visible_to`/`eye_sees`); a ship's `dc_pri` ("", "fire", "flood", "mach") through `dc_k(s, what)`, `flood_magazine(s)`, `counter_flood(s)` (leaves `ballast`, which slows her). Battle keys in use: W A S D, T Q F C V X Z, 0-9, F1-F3, K G B N J, P M R, Tab, Space, Esc.
- **Carrier flying**: aircraft leave and regain the deck singly (`BattleSim.launch_strike`, `update_air`; an air group's `n` is every machine it has, `up` those in the air, `dk` those not yet flown off, `ops` those on the deck run or approach; states `form`, `out`, `home`, `land`; a carrier's `deck_ops`, `deck_t`, `deck_out`; `deck_ok(s)`, `deck_why(s)`, `deck_span(s)`, `wing(s)` for the readouts). Anything that shoots at aircraft must test `up`, not `n`. Every aircraft in the game is drawn by `scripts/plane_art.gd` (`PlaneArt.draw`, loaded by path); no planes are painted on the baked decks. `sink_k(s)` is how far a lost ship has gone down (never read `sink` against a number); `ShipView.heel()` is her list as drawn: a heeled ship is drawn as a solid (owner's decision: if she lists, everything lists) - `Keel` shows her side and red bottom, the deck is carried over by her freeboard, `ship_light.gdshader`'s `lean` extrudes the superstructure from the height map (walls on the high side), and `mount_light.gdshader` does the same for each turret from the height in its texture (per-ship `lean` and `sun_ship` on `ShipView.mount_mat`; a shader helper cannot read `TEXTURE`, pass it as a `sampler2D`); anything new that stands on a ship must take `view.lean` and its height into account. Small shapes in the battle must be drawn about their own place with `draw_set_transform`, not built in world coordinates (they fail to triangulate far from the origin and flicker). `tests/shot_deck.tscn <dir>` shows take-off, landing, a list, torn hull and a sinking.
- **Gunnery and crews** ([`docs/GUNNERY.md`](docs/GUNNERY.md)): a salvo fired at a target (`fire_gun(..., tgt)`) shares the error of the ship's solution on her (`BattleSim.solution`, corrected by `spot` as shells land; `sol_state` for the readout); fire with no target scatters shell by shell as before. A ship record's `xp` is the actions her crew has fought (`Campaign.crew_xp`, `crew_name`; `BattleSim.CREW`, `crew_level`, a `CShip`'s `cq`).
- **Editor mode** (main menu, `GameData.pending.editor`; `scripts/battle_editor.gd`, loaded by path; `tests/shot_editor.tscn` checks it): `BattleSim.editor` (the action never ends), `reveal`, `spawn`, `despawn`, `set_side`, `fit_mounts(s)` (rebuilds a ship's mounts from her record: call `battle.rebuild_view(s)` after), and on a ship `ai_off`, `force_tgt` (any flag: a shell aimed at a ship can hit her whatever her side), `god`. `sim.flag` may be null or an enemy ship there: formations only form on a flagship of side 0. H hides the interface in any battle (`battle.set_clean`) and on the chart. A battle's weather can be changed while it runs: set `sim.wx`, then `battle.apply_weather()`.
- **Splashes and the sea**: a shell's splash is `P.SPLASH` (the hollow, then the column: `shaders/burst.gdshader`) plus `P.WASH` (foam and trough: `shaders/wash.gdshader`, layer "wash"); both take the burst's size on screen in the quad's alpha so tiny ones draw no rings. `tests/shot_splash.tscn` shows them close and far. Reels clips: `tools/make_reels.sh <dir>` (`tests/reel.gd`).
- **Sensors** ([`docs/SENSORS.md`](docs/SENSORS.md)): `BattleSim.has_radar` (no submarines), `phased` (the Aegis research), `eye_sees`, `sonar_range`, `_radar_plot` (a rotating set paints a contact beyond the eye's reach once a turn: `radar_only`, `paint_t`, `paint_pos`); `Campaign.sight_radius`, `found_radius`. R toggles the rings and the sweep on both screens (`Settings.sensors`).

Besides the fields documented above the list: `tech` (a research gate on one design, overriding its class's), `nuc` (burns no fuel), `troops`, `tank`, `ammo`, `repair`, `salvage` (auxiliaries), slot `low` (a turret that stays on the deck behind a raised one). Plan fields: `house` (the long deckhouse), `tubs`, `helo`, `tanker`, `lst`, `school` (a superstructure school other than the yard's, e.g. `tower`), `tubes` and `pod` (submarines), and on flight decks `isl` (leave it out for a flat-top), `sidefun`, `wood`, `edge`. Keep existing design ids: presets, saves and `LEGACY` use them. `tools/bake_assets.js` bakes auxiliaries in every flag's paint.
- **Auxiliaries, troops and prizes** (`campaign.gd`: `bunker_cap`, `_replenish`, `study_prize`; `structures.gd`: `embark`, `invade`, `invade_odds`, `trim_troops`): see [`docs/LAND_STRUCTURES.md`](docs/LAND_STRUCTURES.md) for landings and the roadmap for the rest. A contact's range (`contactKm`) reaches the battle as `range_km` and sets `BattleSim.sep`.
- **Weather and night** (`scripts/weather.gd`, autoload `Weather`; [`docs/WEATHER.md`](docs/WEATHER.md)): nothing is saved, it comes from place and calendar. `Campaign.weather_here()` on the chart; a battle gets `weather` in its setup (`BattleSim.wx`), and `visible_to` decides what a side can see. Tests that are not about weather should set `Weather.force` to a clear day.

- **The game is the Godot 4 project in `godot/`** (owner's decision; the port is complete). GDScript, GL Compatibility renderer.
  - `scripts/campaign.gd` (autoload `Campaign`): campaign state and rules, no drawing. `world_map.gd`: the chart, its panels (ports, command, encounters, aftermath). `main_menu.gd`: title (home page, New campaign page with the origins and test mode). `skirmish.gd`: quick battles.

  - Sea routes run on a precomputed lane network (`data/nav_*.bin`, `nav_routes.json`) through Godot's AStar2D, tied to the start and goal by sight lines over the land mask (`data/land_mask.png`).
- `src/game.html` is the old browser build. It no longer gets features; it stays only as the **art and data source**: `node tools/bake_assets.js` renders every design, paint scheme, mount and effect texture into `godot/assets/` and exports `godot/data/game_data.json` and `sprites.json`; `node tools/bake_world.js` exports the campaign tables (`campaign.json`), then runs `node tools/worldgen/build.js`.

- **The interface** (`scripts/ui.gd`, `UI.setup` from the Settings autoload): Barlow and Barlow Condensed (OFL, `assets/fonts/`) replace the fallback font everywhere, and one theme sits on the root window. Use `UI.title`, `UI.label` (a short all-capitals label at size 12 or less becomes a section header), `UI.button`, `UI.tabs` and `UI.font(...)` rather than styling controls by hand.

- The world map uses 1 map unit = 0.05° (about 5.5 km), equirectangular projection from 82°N to 78°S, wrapping east-west.
