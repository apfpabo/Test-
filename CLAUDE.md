- **Code review round (10 Nov 2026):** what was found, fixed and left for the owner is in [`docs/ROADMAP.md`](docs/ROADMAP.md) ("Code review and bug hunt"). Forces that come looking for you are sized to you: a bounty squadron at `BOUNTY_SIZE` (0.8–2.5) times `strength(G.fleet)` between `BOUNTY_FLOOR` and `BOUNTY_CEIL`, an origin's hunters at `HUNT_K` (1.5) times it, capped by the origin's budget, none before `HUNT_GRACE` (40 days); both built by `Campaign.build_to_strength` (by what ships are worth with their guns, not hull price). Patrols, raiders and the navies are not scaled. Officers draw `HALF_PAY` while you serve no one (`Campaign.half_pay()`: no commission, contract or flag); prize money for a ship sunk is `PRIZE_MONEY` (0.15–0.25) of her worth. `tests/chart_perf.tscn` and `tests/battle_perf.tscn` time the chart and a ten-a-side battle (headless: script time alone).

Armour by place, machinery for a design speed and trim are left open by the owner: do not add them unasked.
- **The economy** ([`docs/ECONOMY.md`](docs/ECONOMY.md)): a port's `short` (per good) and `why`; raise or lower it only through `Campaign.shortage(p, goods, amount, why)`; `Campaign.supply_mult(p, gid)` is everything the world does to a price (war demand, shortage, the flag's works in `G.works`), `econ_day` runs once a day, and a convoy that is destroyed must go through `Campaign.convoy_lost(f)`. Anything that shows or uses a price goes through `world_map.port_price`.
- **Smoke, damage and formations in battle**: smoke is `BattleSim.screens` (round cells; `make_smoke`, `smoke_blocks(a, b)`); anything that asks whether a ship can be seen must go through `visible_to` or `eye_sees`, and anything that hides enemies on screen must test `sim.limited() or not sim.screens.is_empty()`. A ship's `steer` and `list` change how she turns and how fast she goes; `v_cmd` is a speed a station-keeping ship holds (reset each `ai_ship`). A ship of yours may have her own order (`ord`, with `ord_pt` for "goto" and `ord_tgt` for "torp") and her own hold-fire (`hold`): read a ship's order with `ord_of(s)` and her hold-fire with `holding(s)`, never `order` or `hold_fire` directly; the ships picked out in the battle screen are `battle.sel`. Orders: engage, escort, line, abreast, screen, retreat, goto, torp (`station(s)` gives a ship's place in the flagship's frame, x ahead, y to starboard; `keep_station`).
- **Star shell and damage control**: `BattleSim.flares` (`fire_star`, `star_lit(p)`; a ship under a flare at night is seen as by day, through `visible_to`/`eye_sees`); a ship's `dc_pri` ("", "fire", "flood", "mach") through `dc_k(s, what)`, `flood_magazine(s)`, `counter_flood(s)` (leaves `ballast`, which slows her). Battle keys in use: W A S D, T Q F C V X Z, 0-9, F1-F3, K G B N J, P M R, Tab, Space, Esc.
- **Carrier flying**: aircraft leave and regain the deck singly (`BattleSim.launch_strike`, `update_air`; an air group's `n` is every machine it has, `up` those in the air, `dk` those not yet flown off, `ops` those on the deck run or approach; states `form`, `out`, `home`, `land`; a carrier's `deck_ops`, `deck_t`, `deck_out`; `deck_ok(s)`, `deck_why(s)`, `deck_span(s)`, `wing(s)` for the readouts). Anything that shoots at aircraft must test `up`, not `n`. Every aircraft in the game is drawn by `scripts/plane_art.gd` (`PlaneArt.draw`, loaded by path); no planes are painted on the baked decks. `sink_k(s)` is how far a lost ship has gone down (never read `sink` against a number); `ShipView.heel()` is her list as drawn: a heeled ship is drawn as a solid (owner's decision: if she lists, everything lists) - `Keel` shows her side and red bottom, the deck is carried over by her freeboard, `ship_light.gdshader`'s `lean` extrudes the superstructure from the height map (walls on the high side), and `mount_light.gdshader` does the same for each turret from the height in its texture (per-ship `lean` and `sun_ship` on `ShipView.mount_mat`; a shader function cannot read `TEXTURE`: use a macro); anything new that stands on a ship must take `view.lean` and its height into account. Small shapes in the battle must be drawn about their own place with `draw_set_transform`, not built in world coordinates (they fail to triangulate far from the origin and flicker; shells' and tracers' streaks did, until 11 Nov 2026), and a shape that has no length yet must not be drawn at all. `tests/shot_deck.tscn <dir>` shows take-off, landing, a list, torn hull and a sinking. `tests/air_stress.tscn` (headless, about three minutes) fights eight carrier actions and checks every air group's books at every step: run it after touching `update_air` or anything that shoots at aircraft. A battle is not called over while a lost ship is still going down (`step`: `going`). Aircraft whose carrier is lost make for another of their side (`other_deck`) and join her as a guest squadron (`guest` in her `squads`; the campaign keeps no more than her hangar holds). Marks on the upper works are carried over with the plating they are in (`GameData.ship_h_at`). Shells, tracer, missiles and aircraft are drawn in the "overlay" layer with the rings and aiming lines: never hide that layer to hide the interface, test `battle.clean` inside `_overlay` (`set_clean`, H). A staged, captioned clip is its own scene and script (`tests/reel_wolfpack.gd`, `tools/make_wolfpack.sh <dir> <Poppins-Bold.ttf>`: captions in Poppins Bold with a black border at y 330 and 440, fading, and an IN DEVELOPMENT tag); reels pass `quiet` (no siren) and call `Sfx.music("")`. Reels: `tools/make_reels.sh <dir> [clips]` now has `carrier_launch`, `carrier_strike`, `carrier_landing`, `carrier_jets` and `sub_attack` (`tests/reel.gd`: cuts may follow "air", "deck", "torp", "victim", "sub"; `until` runs the action on to the moment the clip opens). Each machine taking off or landing is flown by `BattleSim._fly_op` (an op has `ph` "run"/"fly" or "gate"/"final"/"roll", and its own `pos`, `a`, `v`, `alt`; it turns no faster than an aircraft can): the battle screen only draws it where it is. Water columns ("bursts") are drawn above the ships; a splash against a ship's side sets its foam's `over`, and the "washtop" layer draws that foam over her while it is young (`_wash(true)`). A submerged boat's track is a faint `_ribbon` in the modern style (`P.SWAKE` rings only in the classic). `tests/shot_wash.tscn <dir>` shows these. Never put a `#` comment in the middle of a one-line chain of statements: it cuts off the rest (it cost a battle its readiness charge once).

- **Sound and emissions in battle** (owner's direction, 11 Nov 2026; `battle_sim.gd`): a dived boat is found by sound alone. A ship's `sonar_on` (boats start silent) and `radar_on`; `pings` (rings: `_sonar_tick`, `PING_PERIOD`, `PING_SPEED`, `PING_HOLD`, `PING_HEAR`), a boat's `held_t` (since a ping found her) and any ship's `heard_t` (since an enemy hydrophone heard her ping); `passive_range(listener, boat)` (by the boat's speed; the listener's too); `detected_by` reads only these. `has_radar(s)` is now "fitted and switched on" (`radar_fitted(s)` for the fit alone); a radiating set is picked up `RADAR_HEARD` times as far as it sees by any ship with a set (`_radar_heard`). `visible_to` returns true for a ship whose ping or radar has been heard. The battle screen: right-click a ship of yours for `_ship_menu` (`_sensor_cmd`), Y radar, U sonar; ping rings and echoes are drawn in `_sensors` before the R switch (always shown). `tests/shot_sonar.tscn <dir>`; review_test `_sound`. Keys now in use add Y and U.
- **Depth charges** (reworked 11 Nov 2026 at the owner's word): a pattern is six charges released over time (`drop_dcs`: four off the rails `DC_ROLL` apart, one thrown out on each quarter; entries in `dcs` with `rel` until released), each sinking `DC_SINK` battle seconds before it bursts, so they burst about 100 m astern. A captain drops only on a boat held on sonar and only when the pattern would fall on where she will be (`dc_pattern`, `dc_lead`, `dc_on`), and steers his stern over that place; a burst's damage falls off from `DC_CLOSE` to `DC_HIT`; a hunted boat turns across an attack run and sprints. Measured: a boat takes four to nine patterns from three destroyers; untuned.
- **Collisions and ramming** (reworked 11 Nov 2026 at the owner's word: ramming hurts, friend or foe; `BattleSim._collisions`): a hull is her centreline with half her beam round it (`ram_hull`, `_seg_near`), not a circle. Under `RAM_SLOW` closing speed two ships are only fended off; above it both are hurt by the closing speed and the root of the other's tons (`RAM_K`), the one striking with her stem (the last tenth of her length) at 0.45, the one struck in the side also holed and listing; a boat takes half as much again, and can be rammed down to `RAM_DEEP`. A collision is one event: the pair `sheer` off for 30 s and are not counted again. Captains keep clear of other ships (`_clear_of_ships`, inside `steer_avoid`: a turn under the other's stern, and `brake` for one close ahead); every helm order must go through `steer_avoid`. The flagship is the player's to con.
- **Shells hurt friends** (owner's word, 11 Nov 2026): a shell is tested against every ship where it lands, whatever her flag (only the firing ship is skipped); a captain checks fire when a friend lies within `FRIEND_CLEAR` of the point of aim (`BattleSim.friend_near`; the flagship's hand-laid guns are the player's affair). Guns never engage a dived boat. **Torpedoes and depth charges hurt friends too** (owner's word, 11 Nov 2026): a torpedo strikes any hull in her path (a straight runner passes under a dived boat and never comes back on her firer; a homer runs deaf to `HOMER_SHORT` short of her aim point, then goes for the nearest ship in her seeker's cone, whatever her flag); a burst hurts any boat within `DC_HIT` and any hull within `DC_SHIP` (`dc_harm`), the dropper included if she is slow. Captains and pilots look first: `torp_fouls` (the spread run out against every friend's course; for a homer, a friend she would hear before the target) and `dc_fouls` (each charge's burst against every friend and herself); a ship on a torpedo attack holds, says why (`foul_by`) and gives it up after `TORP_GIVEUP`. The player is warned (`ff_warning`, the red plate under the messages, `battle_hud._ff_plate`) and must press T or Z again within `FF_CONFIRM` (`ff_confirm`). `ff_care` false switches the captains' care off, for `tests/ff_measure.tscn` only. Measures: `sys_log` "torpedo hit"/"torpedo on a friend"/"depth charge hit"/"depth charge on a friend", `ff_dmg_t`, `ff_dmg_dc`.
- **Age, wear and laying up** (Rule the Waves; the owner's go-ahead, 11 Nov 2026; `campaign.gd`): a ship is as old as her design (`built_day`, `age_years`; one bought from a yard has `built` = the day); `wear` grows each day under way (`WEAR_DAY`, faster with age) and costs chart speed (`WEAR_SPEED`, in `fleet_map_speed`) and machinery in battle (`WEAR_ENG`, a `CShip`'s starting `eng`); `overhaul` in port takes it down to `wear_floor`. `lay_up(s, port)` / `recommission(e, port)` / `laid()` (`G.laid`): a laid-up ship is out of `all_ships()` (no crew, supply or wages), pays `LAYUP_FEE` a day, and is seized if her harbour's flag goes to war with you. The port's fleet page has Overhaul, Lay up and a LAID UP list. Arriving in harbour puts the squadron at the port's sea point (`_arrive`), and `set_course` from beside a harbour starts from its roads (a squadron ran into a cove by Bab Hakar and could go nowhere).
- **Wrecks raised after an action** (Starsector; the owner's go-ahead, 11 Nov 2026; `campaign.gd`): holding the field, ships that went down slowly (`WRECK_CHANCE` yours/theirs; never a `CShip.blown` one, never a boat) come back in `apply_battle`'s `r.wrecks`; `recover(w)` (steel and money, `recover_cost`) gives her back at a fifth of her hull with `DEFECTS` in the record's `defects` (shafts: speed in `fleet_map_speed` and `BattleSim.speed_mult`; hull: `max_hull`; fc: gun spread; boilers: `fuel_use`), and `rebuild(s)` in a yard removes them.
