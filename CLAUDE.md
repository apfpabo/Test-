# Iron Meridian — notes for agents

- **Read [`docs/CREATIVE_DIRECTION.md`](docs/CREATIVE_DIRECTION.md) before any design, art, balance or feature work.** It is the project owner's north star. Never edit its text without their instruction.
- **Next task:** the rest of step 5 of the owner's plan: your flag as a power in diplomacy, patrols and convoys of your own, building in ports ([`docs/OWN_FLAG.md`](docs/OWN_FLAG.md), "Not yet"). The first part is done: founding a flag, storming and holding ports (`scripts/flag.gd`, autoload `Flag`; `tests/flag_test.tscn`). A port of yours has `fac == "player"`, and `GameData.fac` gains a `"player"` entry once the flag is founded: loops over `GameData.fac` that mean "the powers" must skip it. Land structures are done: [`docs/LAND_STRUCTURES.md`](docs/LAND_STRUCTURES.md) (`scripts/structures.gd`, autoload `Structures`; tuning in `data/structures.json`; art in `installation_art.gd`, the raid view in `air_raid.gd`; the owner's answers to the brief are recorded there). Diplomacy is done (nine powers, coalitions, sieges, six contract kinds, story chains in `data/stories.json`): [`docs/DIPLOMACY.md`](docs/DIPLOMACY.md) (`scripts/diplomacy.gd`, autoload `Diplomacy`; tables in `data/diplomacy.json`). The fictional world map is done: [`docs/WORLD.md`](docs/WORLD.md).
- **Ship designs** live in `DESIGN_LIST` in `src/game.html` (76 designs, 17 classes). Besides the fields documented above the list: `tech` (a research gate on one design, overriding its class's), `nuc` (burns no fuel), `troops`, `tank`, `ammo`, `repair` (auxiliaries), slot `low` (a turret that stays on the deck behind a raised one). Plan fields: `house` (the long deckhouse), `tubs`, `helo`, `tanker`, `lst`, `school` (a superstructure school other than the yard's, e.g. `tower`), `tubes` and `pod` (submarines), and on flight decks `isl` (leave it out for a flat-top), `sidefun`, `wood`, `edge`. Keep existing design ids: presets, saves and `LEGACY` use them. `tools/bake_assets.js` bakes auxiliaries in every flag's paint.
- **Auxiliaries, troops and prizes** (`campaign.gd`: `bunker_cap`, `_replenish`, `study_prize`; `structures.gd`: `embark`, `invade`, `invade_odds`, `trim_troops`): see [`docs/LAND_STRUCTURES.md`](docs/LAND_STRUCTURES.md) for landings and the roadmap for the rest. A contact's range (`contactKm`) reaches the battle as `range_km` and sets `BattleSim.sep`.
- **Battle effects**: particles are spawned in `battle_sim.gd` (`fx_boom`, `fx_splash`, `fx_muzzle`, enum `P`) and drawn in `battle.gd` (`_parts_modern`, wake ribbons in `_ribbon`); `shaders/bloom.gdshader` is a full-screen pass under the HUD. The classic style must keep working: new particle types are only spawned when `sim.modern`.
- Art pass 2 (turrets, national styles, superstructures) is done; [`docs/NEXT_ART_PASS.md`](docs/NEXT_ART_PASS.md) describes how ship art is now built: height maps baked in the browser renderer and lit in Godot shaders.
- [`docs/LORE.md`](docs/LORE.md) keeps the owner's world-building text word for word (the eight starting origins, and the foundational lore in [`docs/lore/foundational_lore.md`](docs/lore/foundational_lore.md), which `tools/lore_to_json.py` slices into `lore.json` for the codex), how much of it is in the game, and hooks for later. Never edit the owner's text; the in-game copy is `godot/data/lore.json`. Origins are implemented in `godot/scripts/origins.gd`.
- [`docs/ROADMAP.md`](docs/ROADMAP.md) holds the gap analysis, the owner's standing feedback (grounded rather than arcade, Rule the Waves 3 art reference, no boarding) and the order of work. Keep it up to date as features land.
- **The game is the Godot 4 project in `godot/`** (owner's decision; the port is complete). GDScript, GL Compatibility renderer.
  - `scripts/campaign.gd` (autoload `Campaign`): campaign state and rules, no drawing. `world_map.gd`: the chart, its panels (ports, command, encounters, aftermath). `main_menu.gd`: title (home page, New campaign page with the origins and test mode). `skirmish.gd`: quick battles.
  - `scripts/battle_sim.gd`: the battle simulation, no drawing. `battle.gd` draws it and handles input; `battle_hud.gd` is the HUD. Shaders are in `godot/shaders/`.
  - Sea routes run on a precomputed lane network (`data/nav_*.bin`, `nav_routes.json`) through Godot's AStar2D, tied to the start and goal by sight lines over the land mask (`data/land_mask.png`).
- `src/game.html` is the old browser build. It no longer gets features; it stays only as the **art and data source**: `node tools/bake_assets.js` renders every design, paint scheme, mount and effect texture into `godot/assets/` and exports `godot/data/game_data.json` and `sprites.json`; `node tools/bake_world.js` exports the campaign tables (`campaign.json`), then runs `node tools/worldgen/build.js`.
- **The world** (including land structures' places: `LAND_SITES` and `INSTALLATIONS`) is generated and hand-tuned by `tools/worldgen` from `tools/worldgen/world.json` (seed, edits, ports, sites, theatres, names): `node tools/worldgen/build.js` writes `campaign.json` geography, `land_mask.png`, `terrain.png`, the lanes and port routes. See [`docs/WORLD.md`](docs/WORLD.md). Don't reintroduce real place names. Re-run them after changing those in `src/game.html` (then `./build.sh`), and let Godot re-import (`godot --headless --path godot --editor --quit`).
- **The interface** (`scripts/ui.gd`, `UI.setup` from the Settings autoload): Barlow and Barlow Condensed (OFL, `assets/fonts/`) replace the fallback font everywhere, and one theme sits on the root window. Use `UI.title`, `UI.label` (a short all-capitals label at size 12 or less becomes a section header), `UI.button`, `UI.tabs` and `UI.font(...)` rather than styling controls by hand.
- Sound effects are recordings (CC0 plus one CC BY 4.0 engine-room recording) built into `godot/assets/sfx/` by `python3 tools/build_sfx.py <empty download dir>` (needs ffmpeg, numpy, py7zr); sources and licences are in `godot/assets/sfx/CREDITS.md`, and the CC BY credit is on the main menu. Use only CC0, public-domain or CC BY sources and add each to the script's SOURCES. `scripts/sfx.gd` falls back to synthesised sound if a recording is missing. `scripts/settings.gd` (autoload `Settings`) holds volumes (audio buses Effects and Ambience; everything through a fixed 10% output level, `OUTPUT`) and the minimap size in user://settings.cfg.
- The owner plays on **Windows 11**: export with `godot --headless --path godot --export-release "Windows Desktop" ../build/IronMeridian.exe` (template fetch: `tools/fetch_from_zip.py`), zip it and send it.
- Godot checks: `godot --headless --path godot res://tests/run_presets.tscn` plays every preset battle to the end; `res://tests/campaign_test.tscn` checks routes, a month at sea, a battle applied to the campaign and save/load; `res://tests/diplomacy_test.tscn` checks relations, contracts, commissions, wars and saves; `res://tests/waypoint_test.tscn` checks Ctrl+Click waypoints on the chart and in battle; `res://tests/fuel_test.tscn` checks the fuel reserve, dead in the water, a wireless call and a tow; `res://tests/structures_test.tscn` checks land structures (owners, strikes, surrender, income, an assault landing, wars, contracts, saves); `res://tests/escort_test.tscn` checks escorting a fleet from the right-click menu; `res://tests/move_test.tscn` checks chart movement (no sideways slip, speed in a turn, a long passage without grounding); `res://tests/flag_test.tscn` checks founding a flag, storming a port, its yard and dues, relief forces and saves; `res://tests/aux_test.tscn` checks every design's art and fit, troopships and a landing, oilers, replenishment at sea, a prize's drawings and the long-range battle start (`diplomacy_test` takes about ten minutes); `xvfb-run -a godot --path godot --rendering-driver opengl3 res://tests/<shot_battle|shot_map|full_loop>.tscn -- <args>` saves screenshots (`shot_structures` land structures, their panels and an air raid, `shot_bombard` a bombardment, `shot_coast` structures by the coast and a coast course, `shot_mirror` the battle's status panel, `shot_stern` a capital ship's stern under way, `shot_turn` a hard turn at deep zoom, `shot_torps` torpedoes, `shot_context` the right-click menu, `shot_refit` the refit screen, ship systems and the chart's orders box, `shot_fx` captures the battle effects in the modern or classic style, `shot_bwake <dir> [designs]` battle wakes at three zooms, `shot_flag <dir>` your own flag's screens, `shot_troops <dir>` research, troops and a landing, `shot_map` includes close-ups of the Tarsene Gate and the Kessel Canal, `shot_art` frames any designs close up, `shot_designs <out.png>` renders a poster of every design to one scale (`docs/ship_designs.jpg`), `shot_wakes` a fleet under way, `map_zoom_perf` times chart zooming). GDScript can't infer types from Dictionary values, so declare those variables untyped or with an explicit type.
- Ships, factions and names are fictional, though ships are modelled on real designs. A new faction needs entries in `src/game.html` (FAC with an optional `paint` to borrow a baked scheme, NAMES, YARDS, PATROL_POOL, ESCORT_POOL, FAC_MAKERS), `tools/bake_world.js` (MARKET_BIAS), the baked `game_data.json`/`campaign.json`, `Diplomacy.POWERS` and `data/diplomacy.json`. Do not copy assets from Starsector, Rule the Waves or any other game.
- The world map uses 1 map unit = 0.05° (about 5.5 km), equirectangular projection from 82°N to 78°S, wrapping east-west. Always use `wdx`, `mdist` and `mang` for map distances and angles (in Godot: `Campaign.wdx`, `mdist`, `mang`). Use `on_land` for collision, and `set_course` + `route_move` to move fleets.
