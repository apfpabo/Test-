# Iron Meridian — notes for agents

- **Read [`docs/CREATIVE_DIRECTION.md`](docs/CREATIVE_DIRECTION.md) before any design, art, balance or feature work.** It is the project owner's north star. Never edit its text without their instruction.
- [`docs/ROADMAP.md`](docs/ROADMAP.md) holds the gap analysis, the owner's standing feedback (grounded rather than arcade, Rule the Waves 3 art reference, no boarding) and the order of work. Keep it up to date as features land.
- **The game is the Godot 4 project in `godot/`** (owner's decision; the port is complete). GDScript, GL Compatibility renderer.
  - `scripts/campaign.gd` (autoload `Campaign`): campaign state and rules, no drawing. `world_map.gd`: the chart, its panels (ports, command, encounters, aftermath). `main_menu.gd`: title. `skirmish.gd`: quick battles.
  - `scripts/battle_sim.gd`: the battle simulation, no drawing. `battle.gd` draws it and handles input; `battle_hud.gd` is the HUD. Shaders are in `godot/shaders/`.
  - Sea routes run on a precomputed lane network (`data/nav_*.bin`, `nav_routes.json`) through Godot's AStar2D, tied to the start and goal by sight lines over the land mask (`data/land_mask.png`).
- `src/game.html` is the old browser build. It no longer gets features; it stays only as the **art and data source**: `node tools/bake_assets.js` renders every design, paint scheme, mount and effect texture into `godot/assets/` and exports `godot/data/game_data.json` and `sprites.json`; `node tools/bake_world.js` exports coastlines, the land mask, the lane network, port-to-port routes and the campaign tables (`campaign.json`). Re-run them after changing those in `src/game.html` (then `./build.sh`), and let Godot re-import (`godot --headless --path godot --editor --quit`).
- Godot checks: `godot --headless --path godot res://tests/run_presets.tscn` plays every preset battle to the end; `res://tests/campaign_test.tscn` checks routes, a month at sea, a battle applied to the campaign and save/load; `xvfb-run -a godot --path godot --rendering-driver opengl3 res://tests/<shot_battle|shot_map|full_loop>.tscn -- <args>` saves screenshots. GDScript can't infer types from Dictionary values, so declare those variables untyped or with an explicit type.
- Ships, factions and names are fictional, though ships are modelled on real designs. Do not copy assets from Starsector, Rule the Waves or any other game.
- The world map uses 1 map unit = 0.05°, equirectangular projection from 82°N to 78°S, wrapping east-west. Always use `wdx`, `mdist` and `mang` for map distances and angles (in Godot: `Campaign.wdx`, `mdist`, `mang`). Use `on_land` for collision, and `set_course` + `route_move` to move fleets.
