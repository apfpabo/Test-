# Iron Meridian — notes for agents

- **Read [`docs/CREATIVE_DIRECTION.md`](docs/CREATIVE_DIRECTION.md) before any design, art, balance or feature work.** It is the project owner's north star. Never edit its text without their instruction.
- **Next task:** the last two open items from the Starsector comparison in [`docs/ROADMAP.md`](docs/ROADMAP.md): an economy that reacts to wars and sieges, then a supply-based fleet limit instead of the cap of 10 (battles and the HUD assume at most ten ships a side). Step 5 of the owner's plan, your own flag, is done ([`docs/OWN_FLAG.md`](docs/OWN_FLAG.md); `scripts/flag.gd`, autoload `Flag`; `tests/flag_test.tscn`): founding, storming and holding ports, the Chancery (recognition, treaties, peace), works, guard squadrons, convoys. A port or fleet of yours has `fac == "player"`, and `GameData.fac` gains a `"player"` entry once the flag is founded: loops over `GameData.fac` that mean "the powers" must skip it. Land structures are done: [`docs/LAND_STRUCTURES.md`](docs/LAND_STRUCTURES.md) (`scripts/structures.gd`, autoload `Structures`; tuning in `data/structures.json`; art in `installation_art.gd`, the raid view in `air_raid.gd`; the owner's answers to the brief are recorded there). Diplomacy is done (nine powers, coalitions, sieges, six contract kinds, story chains in `data/stories.json`): [`docs/DIPLOMACY.md`](docs/DIPLOMACY.md) (`scripts/diplomacy.gd`, autoload `Diplomacy`; tables in `data/diplomacy.json`). The fictional world map is done: [`docs/WORLD.md`](docs/WORLD.md).
- **Code review round (10 Nov 2026):** what was found, fixed and left for the owner is in [`docs/ROADMAP.md`](docs/ROADMAP.md) ("Code review and bug hunt"). Every fix has a check in `tests/review_test.tscn`; add to it when you fix a defect, and keep its sections counted (a script error ends a section early, and the test fails if a section did not reach its end). `tests/soak_test.tscn -- [days] [origin] [navy] [seed] [v]` plays an unattended campaign (real weather, battles fought by the simulation, a flag, a port, divisions, a tow, strikes, the bomb, saves and loads) and checks the state every day: run a year of it after any change to `campaign.gd`, `flag.gd`, `structures.gd` or `diplomacy.gd`. Every test writes the one save file (`user://iron_meridian_save.json`), so tests that save and load fail falsely when run side by side unless each has its own `XDG_DATA_HOME` (`tools/run_tests.sh` does that). `tests/chart_perf.tscn` and `tests/battle_perf.tscn` time the chart and a ten-a-side battle (headless: script time alone).
- **Things the review settled, to keep:** fleets that have met each other use their own timer `cc` (`cool` is only for contact with the player); a ship bought from a yard carries `paid` (what was put into her: resale and refit refunds are capped by it); a stormed port's structures have `militia` (men who are not yours to embark); `world_map._user_pause` remembers that the player paused the clock; the fleet cap of ten is counted with `Campaign.all_ships()`; `Campaign.fname(f)` gives a fleet's name inside a sentence; the chart's top bar, telegraph and tabs refresh five times a second (`_hud_t`), not every frame, and colour overrides go through `_tc`. In `Campaign.update` nothing is worked out per fleet per tick that can be worked out once (`war`, `ky`, cached `msp`/`mtr`, `route_remaining`'s tail).
- **Decided by the owner after the review:** sighting follows the captain (`Campaign.watch`, `your_watch`, `fleet_watch`; a fleet's `capt`, set in `spawn_fleet`): never give one side a fixed sighting distance the other does not have. `rest_in_port` runs `Campaign.update` with `G.docked` set, so the world moves and no contact is made. Use `Campaign.known_pos(f)` for anything that marks or steers for a fleet the player may not have in sight. `Structures.troops()` and `troop_capacity()` are the soldiers and berths in company; a detached main body keeps its own in its fleet's `troops` (`troops_all()` for pay and food). After removing a ship call `Campaign.trim_cargo()` as well as `Structures.trim_troops()`. `Flag.treaty(fac)` must stop every hostile act of that power. `tools/run_tests.sh <log dir> [tests]` runs the headless tests four at a time, each with its own save folder.
- **Sensors** ([`docs/SENSORS.md`](docs/SENSORS.md)): `BattleSim.has_radar` (no submarines), `phased` (the Aegis research), `eye_sees`, `sonar_range`, `_radar_plot` (a rotating set paints a contact beyond the eye's reach once a turn: `radar_only`, `paint_t`, `paint_pos`); `Campaign.sight_radius`, `found_radius`. R toggles the rings and the sweep on both screens (`Settings.sensors`).
- **Ship designs** live in `DESIGN_LIST` in `src/game.html` (77 designs, 17 classes). Besides the fields documented above the list: `tech` (a research gate on one design, overriding its class's), `nuc` (burns no fuel), `troops`, `tank`, `ammo`, `repair`, `salvage` (auxiliaries), slot `low` (a turret that stays on the deck behind a raised one). Plan fields: `house` (the long deckhouse), `tubs`, `helo`, `tanker`, `lst`, `school` (a superstructure school other than the yard's, e.g. `tower`), `tubes` and `pod` (submarines), and on flight decks `isl` (leave it out for a flat-top), `sidefun`, `wood`, `edge`. Keep existing design ids: presets, saves and `LEGACY` use them. `tools/bake_assets.js` bakes auxiliaries in every flag's paint.
- **Auxiliaries, troops and prizes** (`campaign.gd`: `bunker_cap`, `_replenish`, `study_prize`; `structures.gd`: `embark`, `invade`, `invade_odds`, `trim_troops`): see [`docs/LAND_STRUCTURES.md`](docs/LAND_STRUCTURES.md) for landings and the roadmap for the rest. A contact's range (`contactKm`) reaches the battle as `range_km` and sets `BattleSim.sep`.
- **Weather and night** (`scripts/weather.gd`, autoload `Weather`; [`docs/WEATHER.md`](docs/WEATHER.md)): nothing is saved, it comes from place and calendar. `Campaign.weather_here()` on the chart; a battle gets `weather` in its setup (`BattleSim.wx`), and `visible_to` decides what a side can see. Tests that are not about weather should set `Weather.force` to a clear day.
- **Orders, reserve, divisions, readiness** (`campaign.gd`: `detach`, `rejoin`, `body`, `all_ships`, `readiness`, `rest_in_port`; `battle_sim.gd`: `order` "line", `hold_fire`): use `Campaign.all_ships()` for anything counted over every ship you own (crew, holds, berths), `G.fleet` for the squadron you sail and fight with. **Salvage and running dark**: `campaign.gd` (`salvage`, `salvage_mult`; `signature`, `set_dark`, `heard`; `ck_them` is how far a force finds you). **Sighting**: `Campaign._sighting` sets each fleet's `vis` (and `seenX/Y/Day` for the grey last-known mark); anything on the chart that lists, draws or picks fleets must respect `vis`. **Nuclear weapons**: `structures.gd` (`nuke`, `can_arm`, `_nuke_answers`); a destroyed structure has `nuked`.
- **Structure sites are frozen** in `tools/worldgen/sites.lock.json`: the world builder reads them from there, so a coastline edit does not move them. Delete the file only to choose sites afresh (that breaks saves).
- **Battle effects**: particles are spawned in `battle_sim.gd` (`fx_boom`, `fx_splash`, `fx_muzzle`, enum `P`) and drawn in `battle.gd` (`_parts_modern`, wake ribbons in `_ribbon`); `shaders/bloom.gdshader` is a full-screen pass under the HUD; water columns are `shaders/burst.gdshader`, weather over the battle `shaders/veil.gdshader`. The classic style must keep working: new particle types are only spawned when `sim.modern`.
- Art pass 2 (turrets, national styles, superstructures) is done; [`docs/NEXT_ART_PASS.md`](docs/NEXT_ART_PASS.md) describes how ship art is now built: height maps baked in the browser renderer and lit in Godot shaders.
- [`docs/LORE.md`](docs/LORE.md) keeps the owner's world-building text word for word (the eight starting origins, and the foundational lore in [`docs/lore/foundational_lore.md`](docs/lore/foundational_lore.md), which `tools/lore_to_json.py` slices into `lore.json` for the codex), how much of it is in the game, and hooks for later. Never edit the owner's text; the in-game copy is `godot/data/lore.json`. Origins are implemented in `godot/scripts/origins.gd`.
- [`docs/ROADMAP.md`](docs/ROADMAP.md) holds the gap analysis, the owner's standing feedback (grounded rather than arcade, Rule the Waves 3 art reference, no boarding) and the order of work. Keep it up to date as features land.
- **The game is the Godot 4 project in `godot/`** (owner's decision; the port is complete). GDScript, GL Compatibility renderer.
  - `scripts/campaign.gd` (autoload `Campaign`): campaign state and rules, no drawing. `world_map.gd`: the chart, its panels (ports, command, encounters, aftermath). `main_menu.gd`: title (home page, New campaign page with the origins and test mode). `skirmish.gd`: quick battles.
  - `scripts/battle_sim.gd`: the battle simulation, no drawing. `battle.gd` draws it and handles input; `battle_hud.gd` is the HUD. Shaders are in `godot/shaders/`.
  - Sea routes run on a precomputed lane network (`data/nav_*.bin`, `nav_routes.json`) through Godot's AStar2D, tied to the start and goal by sight lines over the land mask (`data/land_mask.png`).
- `src/game.html` is the old browser build. It no longer gets features; it stays only as the **art and data source**: `node tools/bake_assets.js` renders every design, paint scheme, mount and effect texture into `godot/assets/` and exports `godot/data/game_data.json` and `sprites.json`; `node tools/bake_world.js` exports the campaign tables (`campaign.json`), then runs `node tools/worldgen/build.js`.
- **The world** (including land structures' places: `LAND_SITES` and `INSTALLATIONS`) is generated and hand-tuned by `tools/worldgen` from `tools/worldgen/world.json` (seed, edits, ports, sites, theatres, names): `node tools/worldgen/build.js` writes `campaign.json` geography, `land_mask.png`, `terrain.png`, the lanes and port routes. See [`docs/WORLD.md`](docs/WORLD.md). Don't reintroduce real place names. Re-run them after changing those in `src/game.html` (then `./build.sh`), and let Godot re-import (`godot --headless --path godot --editor --quit`).
- **The interface** (`scripts/ui.gd`, `UI.setup` from the Settings autoload): Barlow and Barlow Condensed (OFL, `assets/fonts/`) replace the fallback font everywhere, and one theme sits on the root window. Use `UI.title`, `UI.label` (a short all-capitals label at size 12 or less becomes a section header), `UI.button`, `UI.tabs` and `UI.font(...)` rather than styling controls by hand.
- Sound effects are recordings (CC0 plus one CC BY 4.0 engine-room recording) built into `godot/assets/sfx/` by `python3 tools/build_sfx.py <empty download dir>` (needs ffmpeg, numpy, py7zr); sources and licences are in `godot/assets/sfx/CREDITS.md`, and the CC BY credit is on the main menu. Use only CC0, public-domain or CC BY sources and add each to the script's SOURCES. `scripts/sfx.gd` falls back to synthesised sound if a recording is missing. `scripts/settings.gd` (autoload `Settings`) holds volumes (audio buses Effects and Ambience; everything through a fixed output level, `OUTPUT`; engines and sea at `Sfx.BED`) and the minimap size in user://settings.cfg.

`arcs_test` is a screenshot scene too and needs an output file). GDScript can't infer types from Dictionary values, so declare those variables untyped or with an explicit type.
- Ships, factions and names are fictional, though ships are modelled on real designs. A new faction needs entries in `src/game.html` (FAC with an optional `paint` to borrow a baked scheme, NAMES, YARDS, PATROL_POOL, ESCORT_POOL, FAC_MAKERS), `tools/bake_world.js` (MARKET_BIAS), the baked `game_data.json`/`campaign.json`, `Diplomacy.POWERS` and `data/diplomacy.json`. Do not copy assets from Starsector, Rule the Waves or any other game.
- The world map uses 1 map unit = 0.05° (about 5.5 km), equirectangular projection from 82°N to 78°S, wrapping east-west. Always use `wdx`, `mdist` and `mang` for map distances and angles (in Godot: `Campaign.wdx`, `mdist`, `mang`). Use `on_land` for collision, and `set_course` + `route_move` to move fleets.
