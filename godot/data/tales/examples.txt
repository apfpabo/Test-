=== requisitioned
title: Space Requested
when: arriving
where: power concord
needs: origin merchant, credits >= 2000
chance: 30%
repeat: no
art: port

--- start
A harbour launch is waiting before your lines are even fast. The officer in it is polite, young and carries a typed
letter: the Concord Admiralty "requests" the use of your after hold for eleven days, to carry crated spares to
{port}'s outstation. Compensation will be paid "in due course".

His sergeant is looking at your cargo manifest as though he has already read it.

* Accept with good grace -> accept
* [credits >= 2000] Find the paperwork mislaid, with a gift for the trouble (2,000) -> bribe
* Refuse: you fly no navy's colours -> refuse

--- accept
The crates are aboard by evening. Eleven days become fourteen, and the compensation, when it comes, is a third of
what the hold would have earned. But your name is now on a list in the right office.
! credits +600
! standing concord +6
* Cast off -> end

--- bribe
The young officer looks hurt; his sergeant pockets the envelope. By morning the letter has been readdressed to a
Syndicate tramp three berths down.
! credits -2000
* Cast off -> end

--- refuse
"As you wish, Captain." He writes something down. You will find the harbour master slower to answer your signals
for a while.
! standing concord -5
* Cast off -> end


=== lights_in_the_rain
title: Lights in the Rain
when: at sea
where: sea Saffron Sea, sea Hesperian Ocean
days: 15+
chance: 4%
repeat: every 90 days

--- start
In a rain squall after midnight the lookouts report a cluster of dim lights, low on the water, holding your course a
mile off the port quarter. No running lights, no answer to the lamp. The wireless room hears short, clipped groups
on a raiders' frequency: they are counting your ships.

* Turn towards them and open fire before they choose the moment -> fight
* Turn away into the squall and run dark -> run
* [credits >= 3000] Signal an offer: a "toll" to pass unmolested (3,000) -> toll

--- fight
! fight raiders pt pt dd "Lantern Wolf" -> won

--- won
Among the wreckage your boats pick up an oilskin pouch: a list of ships and sailing dates, yours among them, and a
name, "the Broker", that means nothing to you yet.
! rp +8
! flag broker_list
* Carry on -> end

--- run
You come round into the thick of the squall at full speed. For an hour the lights hang on; then the rain takes
them. The run costs fuel you had not meant to burn.
! fuel -20
* Carry on -> end

--- toll
A lamp blinks back an acceptance and a bearing. A launch takes the money; the lights fall astern and do not follow.
! credits -3000
* Carry on -> end
