=== lost_squadron_1
title: The Signalman at Halbrook
when: arriving
where: port Halbrook, port Calder Roads, port Halvardsby
needs: origin survivor
days: 5+
art: port

--- start
An elderly petty officer with a knitted scarf and a scarred wrist waits by the harbour steps as {flagship}'s boat makes fast. He looks at your ship's lines for a long moment before speaking.

"You were with the Fourth Division in the Central Hesperian," he says quietly. "I was chief telegraphist aboard the tender at Halbrook the night the wireless went dead. The Admiralty inquiry wrote down enemy submarines. That was a lie."

He glances back toward the customs shed. "I kept a carbon of the last bearing before the flagship broke transmission, Captain. For the price of a passage inland before the Provost's men ask why I am still in port, it is yours."

* [credits >= 1500] Pay his passage and take the carbon copy (1,500) -> buy_bearing
* Press him on duty to the dead without paying -> press_duty
* Walk away: the past has already cost you enough -> decline

--- buy_bearing
He slips a folded wax-paper sheet into your coat pocket. Pencilled in a shaking hand are a latitude in the Central Hesperian, a strange call-sign that is in no fleet list, and a single margin note: "Ordered to hold station by Admiralty cipher 44-Zero."
! credits -1500
! rp +12
! flag halloran_bearing
! log "Bought the final wireless bearing of the Lost Squadron in {port}"
* Return to {flagship} -> end

--- press_duty
He stares at the wet cobbles, then sighs. "We were both there in spirit, I suppose. Take it. Only do not mention my name if you ever take {flagship} out to that meridian."
! rp +8
! flag halloran_bearing
! log "Received the final wireless bearing of the Lost Squadron in {port}"
* Return to {flagship} -> end

--- decline
He nods once, pulls his collar up against the wind, and disappears behind the coaling cranes. Whatever was on that paper stays buried with the rest of the squadron.
* Return to {flagship} -> end


=== lost_squadron_2

where: sea Hesperian Ocean, sea South Hesperian
needs: flag halloran_bearing, day >= 35
chance: 7%

--- start

As your boat hooks the buoy's casing, the radar room calls down: an unmarked destroyer and two fast boats are closing from the mist at twenty-five knots, guns already trained. They have been watching the marker.

The squadron did not vanish into myth. It walked into an ambush, and both admiralties have chosen to bury it.

=== throat_whispers
title: Whispers from the Throat
when: at sea
where: sea Sable Sea, sea Cradle Sea
days: 20+
chance: 5%
repeat: every 90 days
art: contact

--- start
Off the rocky headlands of the inner Sable Sea, your wireless operator takes off his headphones with a frown. A directional burst—short, pulsed telemetry on a pre-armistice Vargan frequency—is coming from the ruined casemates of the Throat Research Anchorage ashore.

Officially, the complex was gutted by bombardment in 1945. Through the glasses nothing moves ashore: no boat under the broken crane, no smoke, no light. Yet the pulses keep perfect time, and a line of low grey buoys rides the swell off the breakwater.

* Send an armed cutter into the cove after dark -> send_cutter
* [standing directorate >= 15] Hail the station using Directorate recognition signals -> hail_station
* Log the coordinates and sell the intercept at the next harbour -> sell_log

--- send_cutter
Your cutter slips past the sunken breakwater at midnight. There is nobody in the ruin. In a sealed casemate a generator the size of a ship's boiler hums with no fuel line and no smoke, warm to the hand, feeding cables that run out under the sea to the buoys. Your men cut loose two relay cabinets and a recording drum, and leave the rest listening.
! electronics +12
! rp +18
! standing directorate -6
! log "Took relay gear from the automated listening station at the Throat Research Anchorage"
* Hoist the cutter aboard -> end

--- hail_station
After a tense silence the station answers with a valid Strelsk naval cipher, too quickly and too evenly for any man's hand: a machine acknowledging a machine. Two days later a Directorate launch meets you offshore. A junior commissar thanks you, coldly, for reporting a fault in a navigation beacon, hands over a set of calibration tables, and pays for your silence about how many buoys you counted.
! rp +15
! standing directorate +8
! credits +2500
* Weigh anchor -> end

--- sell_log
You keep {flagship} well clear of the mined shallows while your wireless room records a full hour of the pulsed signal. Intelligence brokers at the next port pay handsomely for proof that something at the Throat is still listening.
! credits +3500

In a tavern above the mole, a dismissed shipyard draughtsman with a bandaged hand sits alone over an empty glass. He notices your captain's coat and taps a rolled blueprint case against his knee.
