=== squadron_division_rivalry
title: Line of Battle
when: at sea
where: anywhere
needs: ships >= 5, day >= 45
chance: 4%
repeat: every 120 days
art: contact

--- start
During morning manoeuvres in a stiff quartering breeze, your two senior division commanders signal conflicting interpretations of your night-cruising orders—and nearly bring two escorts into collision while jockeying for the van position.

In the wardroom after watches, both officers lay their charts on the green baize table, each convinced the other is endangering the squadron.

* Back the aggressive vanguard captain: speed and shock win actions -> back_vanguard
* Back the cautious screen commander: station-keeping saves hulls -> back_screen
* Put both on watch-and-watch until they learn to manoeuvre as one squadron -> drill_both

--- back_vanguard
The vanguard officers toast your decision in the mess; the screening captains turn in early with tight jaws. Your squadron hits harder on the attack, though discipline frays at the edges.
! rp +10
! fuel -15
! log "Favoured aggressive vanguard tactics during squadron manoeuvres"
* Resume cruising formation -> end

--- back_screen
Order returns to the column before dusk. Ships hold their intervals to the cable, saving fuel and keeping the auxiliaries tucked safely inside the screen.
! fuel +20
! rp +8
! log "Enforced strict station-keeping across the squadron"
* Resume cruising formation -> end

--- drill_both
For forty-eight hours you run the entire squadron through emergency turns, night darkening, and live-fire star-shell drills until every bridge team moves like clockwork.
! fuel -30
! munitions -15
! rp +22
! log "Drilled the entire squadron for forty-eight hours straight"
* Secure from drills -> end


=== squadron_foreign_attaché
title: The Observer's Berth
when: arriving
where: power concord, power directorate, power pacific, power combine
needs: ships >= 6, day >= 70
chance: 20%
art: capital

--- start
A squadron of six hulls flying an independent burgee can no longer slip into {port} unnoticed. Before your anchor chain finishes rattling through the hawsepipe, the local naval commandant sends a polite request: a senior staff captain wishes to sail aboard {flagship} as a "neutral naval observer" for one month.

Everyone in the harbour knows an observer is half diplomat, half spy—but carrying one opens dockyard stores and pays handsomely.

* Welcome the observer aboard and show off your squadron's gunnery -> host_observer
* [credits >= 4000] Host him in luxury ashore while keeping your cipher rooms locked (4,000) -> wine_observer
* Refuse: no foreign uniform walks your quarterdeck -> refuse_observer

--- host_observer
The staff captain takes meticulous notes during every engagement and leaves a glowing report with the Admiralty—along with a generous retainer and upgraded fire-control tables.
! credits +9000
! rp +20
! standing concord +10
! standing pacific +10
! log "Hosted a foreign naval observer aboard {flagship}"
* Weigh anchor -> end

--- wine_observer
Four thousand credits of vintage claret and harbour hospitality keep the observer happily ashore while your squadron refits undisturbed. He writes a warm report anyway.
! credits -4000
! standing concord +8
! standing combine +8
! log "Entertained a naval observer ashore at {port}"
* Weigh anchor -> end

--- refuse_observer
"A private captain's privilege," the commandant replies stiffly. Fuel and ammunition invoices at {port} seem to take twice as long to clear thereafter.
! standing concord -6
! standing directorate -6
! log "Refused a foreign naval observer at {port}"
* Weigh anchor -> end


=== own_flag_embassy_crisis
title: Credentials at the Gate
when: arriving
where: port Tarsa, port Port Saffron, port Kotaraya, port Amakura
needs: ships >= 5, day >= 100
chance: 20%
art: flag

--- start
As {flagship} enters {port}, the harbour saluting battery fires seven guns—not the three accorded to a merchantman, nor the eleven of a sovereign admiral, but the deliberate, ambiguous salute reserved for an armed power the world has not yet decided how to name.

Ashore, envoys from the Tarsene Free Republic and the Meridian Combine both request a private audience at the customs house before you sail. Each wants to know where your guns will point when the armistice finally cracks.

* Promise mutual defence of free sea lanes and sign a commercial accord -> sign_accord
* Demand full belligerent recognition and favourable port dues before committing -> demand_recognition
* Keep both envoys guessing: ambiguity is the sharpest weapon a new flag owns -> stay_ambiguous

--- sign_accord
The agreement is modest on paper, but by evening your ensign flies beside the Republic's and Combine's on the harbour exchange. Trade convoys begin routing through your waters.
! credits +10000
! standing republic +12
! standing combine +10
! log "Signed a maritime accord with free envoys in {port}"
* Return to {flagship} -> end

--- demand_recognition
The envoys balk at your tone, then concede half your terms after glancing out the window at {flagship}'s turrets. Respect at sea is measured in broadsides.
! credits +6000
! rp +15
! standing republic +5
! standing combine +5
! log "Extracted formal maritime concessions in {port}"
* Return to {flagship} -> end

--- stay_ambiguous
Neither envoy leaves satisfied, and both wire their capitals recommending caution—and larger bribes—next time your squadron appears on the horizon.
! rp +18
! log "Kept foreign envoys guessing about your flag's intentions in {port}"
* Return to {flagship} -> end


=== missile_horizon_awakening
title: The End of the Gunline
when: at sea
where: anywhere
needs: era >= 3, day >= 110
chance: 8%
art: bomb

--- start
Forty miles off your starboard beam, a high-altitude plume of white condensation arcs cleanly above the horizon, followed three minutes later by the dull, double thump of a supersonic detonation rolling across the swell.

Your radar plot shows a decommissioned target dreadnought—towed out by naval engineers for classified trials—breaking in half after a single hit from an over-the-horizon guided missile. In the plotting room, your gunnery officer stares silently at his mechanical range-clock. Six centuries of naval warfare have just become obsolete in a single afternoon.

* Send boats into the exclusion zone to sample the missile's seeker wreckage -> sample_seeker
* Hold full-fleet electronic warfare and CIWS drills immediately -> drill_ew
* Signal the test range and offer your squadron as paid picket security -> offer_picket

--- sample_seeker
Your boats recover scorched magnesium fins and an intact active-radar seeker head before the test-range destroyers arrive. Your engineers have their hands on the future.
! rp +50
! electronics +20
! standing concord -8
! standing directorate -8
! log "Recovered active-radar seeker wreckage from a classified missile trial"
* Clear the range -> end

--- drill_ew
You spend the next two days running chaff bursts, radar-silencing drills, and close-in point-defence tracking against target drones. When the missiles fly in earnest, your crews will be ready.
! munitions -20
! fuel -20
! rp +35
! log "Conducted fleet-wide electronic warfare and point-defence drills"
* Resume patrol -> end

--- offer_picket
The test director gratefully accepts: keeping Syndicate snoopers out of the range earns you twelve thousand credits and an invitation to observe the next firing.
! credits +12000
! rp +25
! log "Screened a classified guided-missile test range for hard currency"
* Take station on the perimeter -> end
