=== hijacker_crate_buyer
title: A Quiet Berth in Kesselport
when: arriving
where: port Kesselport, port Port Saffron, port Mbaraka
needs: origin hijacker, flag ashen_crate, day >= 25
chance: 35%
art: port

--- start
Before {flagship}'s gangway is even rigged at {port}, a woman in a waxed linen coat steps aboard without asking leave of the quartermaster. Behind her stand two dockhands carrying a heavy brass-bound sampling case.

"Word travels fast when a stolen freighter puts into a freeport with an extra bulkhead," she says, lighting a thin cheroot. "The Meridian Combine will pay twelve thousand in clean drafts for those gyro-stabilisers—no questions, no bounty hunters in the channel. Or, if you prefer to keep the Directorate's engineers happy, Strelsk's agent ashore will double that in munitions and fuel."

* Sell the crate to the Combine factor and clear your name in Kotaraya (12,000) -> sell_combine
* Trade the crate to Strelsk's agent for war stores and Directorate backing -> sell_directorate
* Keep the crate: when you fly your own colours, your yards will build from it -> keep_tech

--- sell_combine
The sampling case clicks shut over the serial plates. By dusk, the Combine factor's clerk has wired a clean clearance to every Lantern Strait harbour—and your bounty notices quietly vanish from the customs boards.
! credits +12000
! standing combine +18
! standing concord +6
! clear ashen_crate
! flag ashen_settled
! log "Sold the Ashen Wake's prototype stabilisers to a Combine factor in {port}"
* Return to the bridge -> end

--- sell_directorate

! credits +6000
! fuel +60
! munitions +40
! standing directorate +18
! standing concord -10
! clear ashen_crate
! flag ashen_settled
! log "Traded the Ashen Wake's gyro-stabilisers to Directorate agents in {port}"

--- keep_tech
The woman studies your face, snaps her case shut, and nods once. "Bold, Captain. Foolish, perhaps—the Combine's recovery flotilla is already quartering the Saffron Sea."
! rp +25
! standing combine -12
