# Opening tales for six origins (13 Nov 2026).
# The premise of each is the owner's, from section 3 of his narrative bible. The scenes, choices and effects are
# Claude's draft to that premise, for the owner to rewrite: this is not his text. Each comes up once, on the first
# day at sea (or the first arrival) from day 3 on, for a captain of that origin.

=== origin_hijacker
title: The Price of the Ashen Wake
when: at sea
days: 3+
needs: origin hijacker
art: contact

--- start
Three days out, the bosun asks you down to the after hold. A plank of the bulkhead has sprung in the swell, and behind it there is a second wall, newer than the ship.

The ship you stole was not carrying ordinary cargo. Behind the false bulkhead sits a single crate stencilled in Combine script: prototype gyro-stabilisers, packed in grease and horsehair. It explains the bounty notices better than a stolen freighter ever did. Three different flags want this crate back, and none of them will ask politely.

* Keep it hidden and say nothing to the crew -> keep
* Break it open and let your own men study the things -> study
* Heave it over the side: no crate, no reason to hunt you -> ditch

--- keep
You have the plank nailed back and the bosun sworn to silence. The crate is worth more than the ship. Somebody in a freeport will know its price.
! flag ashen_crate
! log "Found a Combine crate of gyro-stabilisers hidden in the hold"
* Carry on -> end

--- study
By lamplight your engineer strips one of the units down on the mess table. He does not understand all of it, but he draws what he sees.
! rp +12
! flag ashen_crate
! log "Opened the Combine crate hidden in the hold and studied what was in it"
* Carry on -> end

--- ditch
It takes four men to get it to the rail. It goes down without a sound worth the name. Whether anyone will believe it is gone is another matter.
! log "Put a Combine crate of gyro-stabilisers over the side"
* Carry on -> end


=== origin_merchant
title: The Requisition Warrant
when: at sea
days: 3+
needs: origin merchant
art: contact

--- start
A cruiser comes up on your quarter at dawn and signals you to heave to. Her boarding officer is polite, tired and in a hurry. He lays a paper on your chart table: an emergency requisition, wartime form, never repealed.

You are to take on ammunition from the cruiser's lighter and carry it through raider waters to the next harbour. The pay is a fixed rate, set in 1943. The escort he promises is one armed trawler.

* Sign it and take the shells aboard -> haul
* Take the shells, lose the trawler in the night, and sell the lot in a freeport -> sell
* Refuse, and cite the armistice -> refuse

--- haul
The lighter is alongside for two hours. Your holds ride low and every man aboard knows what is in them.
! munitions +30
! credits +1500
! flag requisitioned
! log "Requisitioned to carry ammunition through raider waters"
* Get under way -> end

--- sell
The trawler's lamp falls astern a little after midnight. By morning you are alone, with thirty tons of somebody else's shells and a paper that says you are a thief.
! munitions +30
! standing concord -8
! standing freeport +4
! flag requisition_broken
! log "Took requisitioned ammunition and slipped its escort"
* Shape a course for a freeport -> end

--- refuse
The officer reads your face, folds his paper and writes your ship's name in a notebook. "As you like, Captain. We keep lists."
! standing concord -4
* Carry on -> end


=== origin_privateer
title: Ink and Iron
when: arriving
days: 3+
needs: origin privateer
art: port

--- start
A clerk is waiting on the quay at {port} with a summons. Your sponsor's enemies have challenged the legality of your letter of marque before a neutral tribunal, and until it sits your commission is "under review".

The tribunal is three merchants and a retired harbour master in a room above a chandler's. Nobody in it has ever heard a shot fired. The other side has a lawyer.

* [credits >= 2000] Hire an advocate and defend the commission (2,000) -> defend
* Speak for yourself: the paper is good and so are your guns -> speak
* Tear up the summons and walk out -> walk

--- defend
Your advocate talks for an hour about prize law and precedent. The merchants nod. The letter stands, with a seal on it now that it did not have before.
! credits -2000
! standing republic +5
! flag marque_upheld
! log "Defended the letter of marque before a tribunal in {port}"
* Return to {flagship} -> end

--- speak
You tell them what the war was like on the Gate, and what a letter of marque is for. Two of the four believe you. The matter is adjourned, which is not the same as won.
! standing republic +2
! flag marque_adjourned
! log "Spoke for the letter of marque before a tribunal in {port}"
* Return to {flagship} -> end

--- walk
The clerk calls after you down the stairs. By evening the word in every tavern is that your paper is worthless and your flag is whatever you say it is.
! standing republic -6
! standing freeport +4
! flag marque_torn
! log "Walked out of the tribunal in {port}"
* Return to {flagship} -> end


=== origin_defector
title: The Cipher in Your Head
when: arriving
days: 3+
needs: origin defector
art: port

--- start
You are alongside at {port} when the watch finds a man in the wardroom flat who is not on the muster. He has a knife, a Vargan service pistol and no papers. He was looking for your cabin.

Your new patrons have begun to suspect that you did not give them everything when you crossed over. Your old service evidently agrees. Both want what is in your head: one in an interrogation room, the other on the deck.

* Hand him to your patrons' people ashore and tell them the rest of what you know -> tell
* Question him yourself, then put him ashore somewhere quiet -> question
* Say nothing to anyone, and double the watch -> silent

--- tell
They take the man away without a word. Then they sit you down for six hours with a stenographer. When it is over they are friendlier, and you have nothing left to sell.
! credits +3000
! flag cipher_given
! log "Gave the rest of what you knew to your patrons after an attempt on your life in {port}"
* Return to the bridge -> end

--- question
He tells you little, but he tells you who sent him and that he is not the only one. You put him over the side into a fishing boat before dawn.
! rp +8
! flag cipher_kept
! log "Questioned an assassin from the old service in {port}"
* Return to the bridge -> end

--- silent
The crew are told he was a thief. The officers are not fooled. You still hold what both sides want, and now you know they both know it.
! flag cipher_kept
! log "An attempt on your life in {port}; nothing was reported"
* Return to the bridge -> end


=== origin_smuggler
title: The Unmarked Passenger
when: arriving
days: 3+
needs: origin smuggler
art: port

--- start
A Syndicate fixer finds you before your lines are fast at {port}. He offers triple your usual rate to carry one passenger through the Tarsene Gate. No name in the log. No questions at the gangway.

The passenger comes aboard hooded, after dark, with one small case. He speaks to nobody. On the second night your steward hears him muttering figures in his sleep, in Strelsk-accented Vargan: burn times, chamber pressures.

Your cargo is a rocket scientist, and he is running.

* Carry him through as agreed, and take the fee -> carry
* Tell him you know, and ask what the Directorate would pay to have him back -> bargain
* Put him ashore here: this is more than you signed for -> ashore

--- carry
You run him through as you have run everything else. On the far side he shakes your hand, which nobody in this trade does, and is gone.
! credits +6000
! standing freeport +5
! standing directorate -5
! flag passenger_delivered
! log "Carried an unnamed passenger from {port} through the Tarsene Gate"
* Carry on -> end

--- bargain
He goes very still. Then he opens the small case and shows you why he is worth more alive and free than returned: a notebook, close-written, which he lets your engineer copy three pages of.
! credits +3000
! rp +15
! flag passenger_delivered
! log "Carried a defecting rocket scientist out of {port}, and copied part of his notebook"
* Carry on -> end

--- ashore
The fixer is not pleased. The passenger says nothing at all. You watch him walk up the quay with his case, and wonder who picks him up.
! standing freeport -5
* Return to {flagship} -> end


=== origin_academy
title: The Instructor's Wager
when: arriving
days: 3+
needs: origin academy
art: port

--- start
There is a letter waiting for you at the harbour office in {port}, in a hand you know from red ink on your tactics papers.

Your old professor is a desk captain now, and tired of it. He has slipped you an off-the-books patrol sector: raiders working a lane his admirals have written off. He has also, he writes, bet his pension that a green officer with a battered corvette can succeed where their flagships did not. "Do not make me poor."

* Take the sector -> take
* Write back for whatever charts and intelligence he can spare first -> ask
* Decline: you have a ship to keep afloat -> decline

--- take
The sector is a pencilled box on a chart and a list of sinkings. It is not much. It is the first order anyone has given you that was meant to be carried out.
! flag instructors_wager
! log "Took an off-the-books patrol sector from an old instructor"
* Return to {flagship} -> end

--- ask
A week's worth of signals logs arrives by the next mail packet, with his notes in the margins. He has been thinking about these raiders for some time.
! rp +8
! flag instructors_wager
! log "An old instructor sent his notes on a raider lane"
* Return to {flagship} -> end

--- decline
You write a careful letter. The reply, when it comes, is one line: "Then I shall bet on someone else."
* Return to {flagship} -> end
