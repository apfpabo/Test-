=== vardy_1_gate
title: The Man on the Strelsk Cruiser
when: at sea
where: sea Cradle Sea, sea Hesperian Ocean, near Gatehold 400
days: 20+
chance: 4%
art: contact

--- start
In the grey chop off the Tarsene Gate approaches, a heavy Directorate cruiser alters course to parallel {flagship} two miles to port. Her signal lamp flashes with crisp, unhurried precision:

"From Commodore Tomaž Vardy, commanding *Perun*. Your handling in the swell is credit to your service, Captain. Enjoy the armistice while the politicians keep it—when the whistles blow again, I look forward to measuring your armour against Strelsk steel."

* Dip your ensign in professional courtesy and hold course -> salute_vardy
* Train your main turrets onto his bridge to test his nerve -> train_guns
* Signal an invitation to parley by boat while the truce holds -> parley_vardy

--- salute_vardy
Vardy's cruiser dips her red-and-iron ensign in return and hauls off toward the Strelsk Sounds. A worthy adversary has entered your name in his private log.
! standing directorate +4
! flag met_vardy
! log "Exchanged naval courtesies with Commodore Tomaž Vardy"
* Steady on course -> end

--- train_guns
For thirty seconds, two dozen heavy barrels track each other across the grey water. Neither fires. Vardy flashes a single word—"Noted"—before sheering away into the mist.
! rp +8
! standing directorate -6
! flag met_vardy
! log "Faced down Commodore Tomaž Vardy in a tense gunnery standoff"

--- parley_vardy

! rp +15

=== maren_1_saffron
title: The Ledger of Selene Maren
when: arriving
where: port Port Saffron, port Kesselport
days: 18+
chance: 20%
art: port

--- start

"Every captain who survives six months in these waters eventually buys information from me, Captain," she says, tapping a stack of decoded Admiralty signals. "Give me a modest retainer today, and whenever a bounty hunter or naval task force takes a contract on your head, my wireless operators will warn you a week before they clear the breakwater."

* [credits >= 3500] Pay Selene Maren's intelligence retainer (3,500) -> pay_maren
* Offer to pay in salvaged technical data instead of coin -> trade_maren

! credits -3500

! log "Retained intelligence broker Selene Maren in {port}"

--- trade_maren
Maren examines your captured fire-control sketches through a jeweller's loupe and smiles. "Better than silver. Consider yourself on my protected list."
! rp -10
